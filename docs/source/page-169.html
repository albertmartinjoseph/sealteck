The Sectors We Serve

At Sealteck Sealing Solutions Limited, we provide high-performance sealing solutions designed to ensure safety, reliability, and compliance across a wide range of demanding environments. From heavy industry to critical infrastructure, our tailored seals protect your assets, lower your total cost of ownership, and keep your operations running smoothly.

Infrastructure

Safe, reliable seals for a sustainable society.

We provide critical sealing solutions that protect vital public and private infrastructure from water, fire, gas, and environmental hazards.

  • Advanced Facilities & Data Centers: Protecting sensitive equipment and ensuring uninterrupted operations.
  • Transportation: Bridges, road tunnels, rail infrastructure, airports, and ports.
  • Construction & Buildings: Commercial buildings and civil construction projects.
  • Utilities & Telecom: Water management facilities and telecommunication networks.

Manufacturing Industry

Superior solutions as your manufacturing standard.

Our solutions enable high manufacturing quality for both Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and end-users, ensuring that complex machinery remains protected and operational.

  • Food & Beverage: Hygienic and compliant sealing for processing facilities.
  • Robotics & Automation: Protecting sensitive cabling and moving components.
  • Rolling Stock: Durable seals for trains and railway vehicles.
  • Defense Vehicles: Ruggedized protection for military and defense transport.

Marine

Extend the lifecycle of your vessels.

Whether it is a new-build project or a retrofit, our cable and pipe transits secure watertight, fire-rated integrity across all types of vessels at sea.

  • Commercial Shipping: Carriers, cargo ships, and offshore support vessels.
  • Passenger Vessels: Cruise liners, ferries, and luxury yachts.
  • Defense: Specialized sealing for naval vessels.

Offshore Oil & Gas

Uncompromising safety for extreme environments.

Our cable transits and pipe seals are the key to safety and reliability in one of the world’s most demanding sectors, extending the lifecycle of your offshore units.

  • Rigs & Platforms: Semi-submersible rigs and jack-up rigs.
  • Vessels: Drill ships, offshore support vessels, and FPSOs (Floating Production Storage and Offloading).

Power Generation & Distribution

Entry solutions for a stable energy supply.

We secure energy operations with robust seals that handle the unique demands of both traditional power generation and the rapidly expanding renewable energy sector.

  • Renewable Energy: Onshore and offshore wind turbines, solar plants, and energy storage facilities.
  • Traditional Power: Gas turbine power plants and nuclear facilities.
  • Transmission & Distribution: AC/DC transmission networks, HVDC, and local power distribution.

Process Industries

Solutions for safe, continuous operations.

Process facilities require heavy-duty sealing to manage harsh chemicals, extreme temperatures, and hazardous environments. We provide the transits needed to keep these plants safe.

  • Mining & Metals: Heavy-duty protection for extraction and refinement facilities.
  • Onshore Oil, Gas & Petrochemical: Securing pipelines, refineries, and processing plants.
  • Pulp & Paper: Moisture and chemical-resistant sealing for continuous processing lines.

About

At Sealteck, we didn’t just see a gap in building entries; we saw a critical flaw in industry standards. Having spent years witnessing the costly consequences of inferior sealing products and substandard workmanship across various sectors, we decided it was time to redefine how critical infrastructure is protected.
